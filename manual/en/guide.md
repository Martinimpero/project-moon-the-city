# THE CITY: A PLAYER'S GUIDE

*Everything a player needs, in one place: the City and how its people live, your character sheet shown as it is, how every rule you will touch works (E.G.O., Stress, Harm, Bonds, the Sins, fights), and how to build a character. Read the first part before you create anyone; keep the rest at the table. Everything about the City is a starting point: your GM may swap any name for one of their own.*

## 1. Where you are

The City is enormous, old and layered: a corporate tower can stand two streets from a district the utilities forgot. Nobody sees all of it. It is split into Districts, and each one belongs to a **Wing** (a corporation). Every District has two faces: the **Nest**, its walled and protected corporate core, and the **Backstreets** around it, where the Wing rarely bothers to manage anything directly. Beyond the walls are the **Outskirts**, where the Head sends whatever it will not have inside. (The **City** button in the app shows the map of the Districts.)

**When you play.** The game starts just after the **White Nights and the Dark Days**: seven days when the City lit up and then went dark. Since then people have begun to **Distort**, and a few have answered with **E.G.O.** Both are recent, rare and badly understood. Most Cityfolk have never seen either, and nobody trusts what they are told.

**Numbness.** Loss is common here, so people rarely hold funerals, keep their distance and avoid strong ties. Fixers die young and know it. That is why a **Bond** matters more here than it would anywhere else, and why a scene where someone lets another person get close should be allowed to land.

## 2. Who runs things

| Power | What it is | How it presses on you |
|---|---|---|
| **The Head** | Sits above everything and is never seen. It sets the Taboos and steps in only when one is broken. | Like weather: a file that goes missing, an order that overrides a sensible decision. It does not negotiate or explain. |
| **Wings** | Corporations built around a **Singularity**, a contained piece of something not fully of this world. Each holds a District. | Through **Employment**: a Bond that cuts both ways. They pay well and protect their own, which makes it hard to spend a Bond against them. |
| **Syndicates** | They run the Backstreets day to day, on favors, debt and territory. The five biggest are the **Five Fingers**. | Through **Debt** and **Obligation**: Bonds owed to people who collect without much restraint. |
| **Offices** | Small business outfits where most working Fixers actually punch in. | Through the daily grind: quotas, a cut off every payment, and whether the person who hands out work still trusts you. |
| **Associations** | Twelve formal bodies that license and grade Fixers. The largest and most trusted is the **Hana Association**. | Through the licence and the **Report** you file after every job. Standing lost with one follows you everywhere. |

## 3. How people live

**In a Nest.** Safe, well paid and watched. The Wing protects its people with real medical care and real counsellors, and asks for a loyalty that looks a lot like family right up until it doesn't. Nests are made of **feathers**: the people the Wing protects, taxes and may evict. Every Singularity runs on somebody's suffering, and the staff may or may not know whose.

**In the Backstreets.** Market stalls under damp scaffolding, deals struck on credit, and a Syndicate that "lends" to anyone who looks like they are struggling and always collects. A **migration permit** from the Backstreets to a Nest is a dream prize, and also a leash: the Wing can take it back. The Backstreets are also where the Night is worst.

**As a Fixer.** You take jobs nobody official wants to sign: recovery, negotiation, investigation, protection, removal. Most Fixers work through an **Office**, which keeps a cut of every payment (the Ninth Registry Office keeps 30%, by a formula nobody has seen written). An *Associate* Office cannot refuse the work it is given. Independents are free, rare and rarely last. You carry a **Grade** from 9 (least proven) to 1, earned only by jobs that were actually done and vouched for. Most jobs are **Contracts** with a Risk from 1 to 4, and by the time one reaches you the client's name is usually the least true thing about it.

**Everyday life.**
- **Money** is Ahn. Most people spend nearly all of it on living costs and taxes. In the game it is your **Resources** (0 to 5).
- **Augments** (prosthetics and upgrades) are almost universal among Fixers. Nest augments are good and expensive; Backstreets augments are cheap and have side effects. Only the brain stays organic. An augment is also a debt to whoever made it.
- **Workshops** make gear and run on patents. The best ones choose their clients, and a no-name Grade 9 may be refused.
- **The Outskirts** are where the Head dumps what it will not have inside. People nobody wants end up there.

## 4. The Night and the Taboos

**The Night.** Every night, for about eighty minutes (roughly 3:13 to 4:34), the Backstreets lose almost all law. Two rules hold: **residential buildings are not touched**, and **nothing may be recorded** (only eyewitness testimony counts, and reporting what happened draws the attention of the Claw). The Head's **Sweepers** come through in three waves and consume everything left outdoors. Criminals use the Night to dispose of people. Know where your character sleeps, and where they are at 3 a.m.

**The Taboos.** The Head's laws are few and absolute. Examples: human-like machines and AI, resurrection, cloning beyond seven days, limits on firearms, tax evasion, and breaking the rules of the Night. A Wing may add its own **house Taboos** inside its District. Breaking one brings a **Taboo Hunter**: a Fixer hired by a Wing to stop the infraction and recover the evidence, with lethal force allowed.

## 6. Your character sheet

This is the sheet you will fill in, shown exactly as the app prints it (a filled-in example, Wren Okoro, one of the pregenerated characters). The app shows the same fields on screen, and the manual's Appendix A has a blank one.

*(The PDF version of this guide prints a filled-in sheet here. In the app, open any character to see the real one.)*

### How to read it, from top to bottom

**The identity lines.** Eight of these describe who the character is and never change numbers: **Concept** (one line), **Identity** (how you see yourself), **Background** (where you come from), **Occupation** (how you earn), **Affiliation** (who you work for), **Relationship** (the person who matters to you), **Principle** (a belief that can bend) and **Ambition** (the question your story answers). The other four do real work at the table:
- **Desire.** Something concrete you could chase this week. The GM leans on it.
- **Fear.** Your specific fear, part of your Wound. It is what the Verdict says to you at the Hail Mary.
- **Vice.** A recurring weakness. The GM can invoke it for a Complication, and you regain 1 E.G.O. (once per scene). It is also what takes the wheel in a Flashpoint.
- **Boundary and Burden.** The other two parts of your Wound. The **Burden** is a past event written as a trigger (your first Trauma). The **Boundary** is the line you will not cross again; it decides when the Hail Mary happens.

**Attributes** (Body, Mind, Presence, Resolve, 1 to 5) and **Skills** (twelve, 0 to 5) are the dots you add to make a dice pool. **Resonance** has seven Sins from 0 to 4 and tells you which Sins you can tag and which is your **Alignment**.

**E.G.O., Stress, Resources, Grade.** Your current E.G.O. over your maximum, your Stress from 0 to 5, your money (0 to 5) and your Grade.

**Harm.** Four boxes: Hurt, Injured, Wounded, Maimed/Dying. You tick them as you take Harm.

**Gear and Bonds.** Each piece of gear shows its kind, its Sin and its **Wear** (0 to 3). Each Bond shows its Type and Strength.

**Trauma and Scars.** The Trauma you started with from your Burden, and any Trauma added later. Scars appear after a Distortion.

## 7. How the game works

### The roll

When your character tries something with an uncertain result and real consequences, you roll.

1. **Build the pool.** Add one **Attribute** and one **Skill**: that many d10. (No points in the Skill? Roll the Attribute alone.)
2. **Count Successes.** Every die showing **7, 8, 9 or 10** is a Success. Nothing else counts.
3. **Compare to the Difficulty,** which the GM states **before** you roll.

| Difficulty | What it means |
|---|---|
| 1 | Routine, but not risk-free. |
| 2 | Genuinely challenging, professional level. |
| 3 | The City actively pushing back: dangerous, expert level, or opposed by someone competent. |
| 4+ | Exceptional. Needs preparation, Help or E.G.O. to be reachable. |

| Successes | Result |
|---|---|
| Difficulty + 2 or more | **Critical Success**: full effect plus something extra. |
| Difficulty or more | **Success**: you get what you wanted. |
| Difficulty − 1 | **Partial**: "you do it, but..." (at a cost) or "you fail, and..." (it gets worse). |
| Difficulty − 2 or less | **Failure**: it does not work, and the scene moves on with a real consequence. |
| 0 Successes and at least one die showing 1 | **Critical Failure**: something goes actively wrong. Checked first. |

At Difficulty 1 there is no Failure band: the worst ordinary result is a Partial. **A failure never means "nothing happens".**

**Declare the stakes.** Before a consequential roll, the GM says what a Success gets, what a Partial costs and what a Failure changes. If you are not told, ask.

**Opposed rolls.** When someone resists, both sides roll a pool and compare Successes from the point of view of whoever is **trying to change things**: margin +3 or more is a Critical Success, +1 or +2 a Success, 0 or −1 a Partial, −2 or worse a Failure. A tie favors whoever is not acting.

**Helping.** Another character can help your roll: they describe what they do and roll their own pool at **Difficulty 1**; **each Success adds one die** to yours. You can be helped once per roll, and the helper shares the consequence if it goes wrong.

**Clocks.** Pressure that should build over several scenes is tracked on a **Clock** of 4, 6 or 8 segments. One segment fills per significant setback (a failed roll, a Complication), never more than two at a time. When it fills, something concrete happens (an audit, a visit, a contract pulled).

### E.G.O.

E.G.O. is a pool from 0 up to your **Resolve**: how much of yourself you can consciously draw on right now.

- **Spend it.** Before a roll, spend E.G.O. points one for one as **extra d10s**. They count as Successes like any die.
- **Complications.** Any E.G.O. die showing a **1 or 2** brings a **Complication**, a concrete cost the GM adds whether you win or lose. On a roll tagged with a Sin, it is that Sin's Complication.
- **Recover it.** A full night of rest gives back 1; a real downtime scene gives 1 more. At most **2** between Contracts. Refunds from your Vice, Sin techniques and gear together give at most **2 per scene**.
- **Forced loss.** Interrogation, psychological attack or horror can take E.G.O. directly. At Stress 2 or more it takes 1 extra; at Stress 4 or more, 2 extra.

**Running low.**

| State | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Steady** | E.G.O. at half your maximum or more. | Nothing special. |
| **Unsteady** | Below half (judged at the start of the roll, before spending). | The first die showing a **1** in any roll each scene triggers a **Flashpoint**. |
| **Empty** | 0 E.G.O. | **Every roll** showing a 1 triggers a Flashpoint (one per roll). |

A **Flashpoint** is a beat in which your **Vice** (or your Sin's Urge) takes the wheel. The GM narrates it, you play through it. If your maximum is 2 or less you skip Unsteady and go straight to Empty. If E.G.O. would hit 0 at a moment that touches your Boundary directly, that is a **Hail Mary**.

**The Voice.** A quiet voice only you hear, offering a reading of your life. It starts when you are Unsteady and grows louder when Empty. Its shape follows your highest Attribute: **Body** lashes out, **Mind** withdraws or sabotages, **Presence** performs or provokes, **Resolve** digs in.

### Stress

Stress goes from 0 to 5 and measures how close your fault line is to breaking, across the whole campaign. It almost never goes down.

| Raises Stress | By |
|---|---|
| A Trauma trigger firing, a stated Boundary violated, a Critical Failure, a Bond lost outright, an E.G.O. Manifestation | +1 |
| A Distortion | +2 |
| Landing a serious blow or causing real Harm to another person (once per scene, unless it is already clean for you) | +1 |

**Lowering it.** Downtime spent on a healthy coping activity tied to a Bond lowers Stress by 1. An unhealthy version also lowers it by 1 but strains a Bond. Either works **once between Contracts**. Beyond that, only a **Reckoning** lowers it.

| Stress | Effect on your E.G.O. maximum |
|---|---|
| 0 to 1 | none |
| 2 to 3 | −1 |
| 4 to 5 | −2, and **Fraying**: every Hail Mary roll gets +1 Difficulty |

Your maximum never drops below 1.

### Harm

There are no hit points. Harm goes on a four-step track, filled in order when you are hurt in a scene the GM framed as truly dangerous.

| Tier | Meaning | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Hurt** | Bruises, a scare. | No penalty. Clears at the end of the scene. |
| **Injured** | A real wound. | −1 die on rolls the injury affects, until treated. |
| **Wounded** | Serious harm. | −2 dice, and it worsens unless it gets real medical attention. |
| **Maimed / Dying** | Out of the scene. | You and the GM decide the lasting consequence, or death if the fiction supports it. |

**Healing.** Harm heals one tier at a time from the top. **Injured** needs treatment (Mind or Resolve + Medicine, Difficulty 1, with a kit or clinic), then heals a tier each downtime phase. **Wounded** must be treated by the end of the next scene (Medicine, Difficulty 2, Help allowed) or the next hit makes you Maimed. **Maimed** must be treated by the end of the next scene (Medicine, Difficulty 3) or you die; if you survive you are Wounded plus a lasting consequence. Taking a hit while Maimed kills. Wing Support heals one extra tier per downtime phase.

### Bonds

A **Bond** ties you to a person. It has a **Type** (Trust, Fear, Debt, Affection, Obligation or Hatred) and a **Strength** from 1 to 3. You can hold several Bonds, of different types, with the same person.

**Spend 1 point of Strength to:**
- add a bonus die to a roll where the relationship plausibly helps;
- upgrade a Partial to a full Success (once per scene);
- resist 1 point of Stress the relationship would reasonably cushion.

Spending does not destroy a Bond, but it does not refill by itself: you recover it with a real scene that cares for the relationship. A Bond **grows** +1 (to 3) through a scene dedicated to it, and **decays** if spent hard or neglected. Bonds run **both ways**: the GM can spend a Bond an NPC has on you to call in a favor or justify a betrayal.

**The crew's own Bond.** The team has a Bond with itself (Trust or Obligation, Strength 1 to 3, starting at 2). Any member can spend a point to add a die when another member Helps. At 0 the crew is **Fractured**: no Help between members until a scene with everyone brings it back to 1.

### Vice, Trauma and your Boundary

- **Vice.** At any time the fiction allows, the GM can invoke your Vice to bring a Complication into the scene. **You regain 1 E.G.O.** (once per scene). You may also invoke it yourself with the GM's approval.
- **Trauma.** When a Boundary or Fear that has no Trauma yet is hit hard enough, it creates one: a one-line trigger written by you and the GM. Every time that trigger happens, **Stress rises by 1** and you must narrate a brief, specific reaction (a habit, a flinch, a thing you say or cannot say). It is not optional color.
- **A second fault line.** After the same Trauma has fired **three times**, you may write a second Boundary from it, with the GM's agreement. You can hold up to three Boundaries over a campaign.

### The Hail Mary

When your E.G.O. would hit 0 at a moment that hits your **Boundary** directly, you make your **Hail Mary**. You may also **dig deep**: spend all your remaining E.G.O. (at least 2) to trigger it earlier, at +1 Difficulty (not available on a fault line with an unhealed Scar).

1. **The Verdict.** A voice only you hear tells you what your Wound has always said about you, in the words of your Fear. The GM says it. It should hurt.
2. **Your Acceptance.** You answer: you deny the Verdict and say, in your own words, who you are underneath. The moment needs a sincere Desire that your Boundary now contradicts.
3. **The roll.** **Resolve + a relevant Skill**, Difficulty 2 or 3, tagged with the Sin of the crisis. You may spend a Bond for +1 die. Each Boundary you already broke this campaign gives −1 die. If you cannot say your Acceptance, a Success or Critical Success becomes a Partial Distortion.

| Result | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Critical Success** | E.G.O. Manifestation, clean. E.G.O. refills to 1. Stress +1. |
| **Success** | Manifestation at a cost: a hard price in the fiction (a Trauma marked, a Boundary bent). Stress +1. |
| **Partial** | **Partial Distortion**: a visible, monstrous transformation, but you keep playing the character. Stress +2. |
| **Failure** | **Complete Distortion**: the self is pushed out, the Distortion's own wants take over. Stress +2. |
| **Critical Failure** | Complete Distortion, already critical: death or a permanent change are live options from the first moment. |

**E.G.O. Manifestation.** Your identity made momentarily solid: a weapon, armor, eyes that catch light wrong, a voice that carries. It lasts one scene and looks like your Sin. If you Manifest successfully a **second** time on the same fault line, it becomes a **Permanent E.G.O.**: a named ability with a physical form, always available, costing 1 E.G.O. to activate. It closes that fault line to Distortion.

**Distortion.** You become something else, and it is physical and dangerous. **Partial:** you keep the wheel and play your character as the monster. **Complete:** the Distortion drives; the GM may speak for you (and should ask first), but the person is still reachable. There is no timer and **no recovery roll**: you come back when something in the fiction reaches you (a Bond, the one true thing, someone who will not give up on you). Afterwards you carry a **Scar**: a **Tell** (a visible change) and the Boundary that broke, marked **Broken** (you cannot spend a Bond for a die on it and rolls that invoke it take −1 die) until it Heals. Another Complete Distortion on a Scarred fault line means you die or go **Fallen**.

**The Reckoning.** Between sessions you can ask for a scene where you confront your wound with the Bond that matters most. It must be played, and the GM judges whether it engaged the wound. On success **Stress drops by 1**, and if it confronted the cause of a Scar, the Scar **Heals**.

### The Sins in play

You have a **Resonance** from 0 to 4 in each of the seven Sins. Your highest is your **Alignment**.

- **Tagging.** You can **tag** any roll with a Sin you have at **2 or more** if you can say how the action expresses it ("I hit him because I am furious"). One tag per roll, never compulsory. The GM can veto a tag the fiction does not support.
- **The Wheel.** Each Sin is strong against the next two and weak against the previous two, in this order: **Sloth, Wrath, Pride, Envy, Gluttony, Lust, Gloom**, and back to Sloth. Your tag is compared with the other side's tag or, if they did not tag, with their **Alignment**.

| Matchup | Your pool |
|---|---|
| Strong | +1 die (+2 at rating 4, +3 at rating 5) |
| Weak | −1 die (−2 at rating 4, −3 at rating 5) |
| Neutral, same Sin, no tag, or rating 1 | no change |

| Sin | Strong against | Weak against |
|---|---|---|
| Sloth | Wrath, Pride | Gloom, Lust |
| Wrath | Pride, Envy | Sloth, Gloom |
| Pride | Envy, Gluttony | Wrath, Sloth |
| Envy | Gluttony, Lust | Pride, Wrath |
| Gluttony | Lust, Gloom | Envy, Pride |
| Lust | Gloom, Sloth | Gluttony, Envy |
| Gloom | Sloth, Wrath | Lust, Gluttony |

- **Fit and Riding.** Being **Under** a Sin helps some Skills and hurts others. At rating 2 or more: the Skills it favors get **+1 die**, the Skills it hinders get **−2 dice** (+2 and −3 at rating 4). You are Under a Sin when you **Ride** it (declare it at the start of a scene, free, one per scene: every roll that scene counts as tagged, but the Hindered Skills hurt), when its Urge took your Flashpoint (for the rest of the scene), or while Distorted. Matchup and Fit together never go above +3 or below −3.

| Sin | Favored (+1) | Hindered (−2) |
|---|---|---|
| Wrath | Combat, Athletics, Persuasion | Stealth, Investigation, Medicine |
| Lust | Persuasion, Empathy, Deception | Investigation, Technology, Corporate Knowledge |
| Sloth | Stealth, Medicine, Investigation | Athletics, Combat, Streetwise |
| Gluttony | Investigation, Streetwise, Corporate Knowledge | Stealth, Empathy, Medicine |
| Gloom | Empathy, Medicine, Investigation | Persuasion, Deception, Athletics |
| Pride | Persuasion, Corporate Knowledge, Fixer Knowledge | Empathy, Stealth, Deception |
| Envy | Deception, Stealth, Technology | Persuasion, Medicine, Fixer Knowledge |

- **Signature Technique.** With rating 2 or more you unlock your Sin's technique, once per scene (twice at rating 4):

| Sin | Technique | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Wrath | **Rampage** | After a Wrath-tagged Success in a fight, make a second Wrath-tagged attack on another target in the same Exchange at −1 die. If either roll shows a 1, an unintended person takes Hurt. |
| Lust | **Hook** | After a Success on a Lust-tagged social roll, the target holds a 1-point Bond toward you until the scene ends, which you may spend once. |
| Sloth | **Unmoved** | As your action, do nothing: until your next action, attacks and pressure against you are at −2 dice and you cannot be Helped. |
| Gluttony | **Devour** | When you inflict Harm or win a Social Conflict, regain 1 E.G.O. or take something; then the scene costs you something. |
| Gloom | **Sorrow's Weight** | After a Success on a Gloom-tagged roll, the target takes −2 dice on its next roll this scene. |
| Pride | **Unbowed** | After rolling, reroll up to three dice that did not succeed. If it still fails, take a Complication. |
| Envy | **Borrowed Face** | After your opponent tags a Sin, tag the same one: the matchup is neutral and you add +1 die. |

- **Complications and the Urge.** On a Sin-tagged roll, an E.G.O. die showing 1 or 2 brings **that Sin's Complication** (Wrath: collateral damage; Lust: an attachment or a strained Bond; Sloth: delay; Gluttony: excess and a trace; Gloom: your sorrow costs an ally a die; Pride: loss of face; Envy: a new rival). A Flashpoint is your Vice or your Sin's **Urge** (Wrath lashes out, Lust clings, Sloth freezes, Gluttony takes too much, Gloom collapses inward, Pride refuses help, Envy copies and sabotages).
- **Drift.** You keep a **Sin Tally** of tagged rolls. At the end of every **fourth session**, if your most-tagged Sin leads the next by **8 or more**, it gains +1 rating (up to 4) and your least-tagged Sin loses 1. You become what you do. A Reckoning that confronts that Sin can move a point back. At rating 4 you are **Overtaken** (your Urge always takes your Flashpoint); rating 5 (**Saturated**) is a deliberate, risky choice.

### Fights

A scene becomes **Combat** only when someone acts to cause physical Harm, or credibly threatens it, and someone is willing and able to resist by force. Everything short of that is a Social Conflict or a single roll. The GM says it out loud: "this is a fight now".

- **Exchanges.** Combat is not fixed turns. In each **Exchange** every character takes one significant action, in an order the GM sets from the fiction (an ambush, a faster draw). There is no Initiative.
- **Attacks.** An attack is an opposed roll: **Body** (melee, thrown) or **Mind** (aimed ranged) **+ Combat**, against the defender's **Body + Athletics** (dodge) or **Body + Combat** (block). The attacker needs **more Successes** to hit cleanly. A **Partial** is a Complication or a Hurt; a **Success** advances Harm one tier; a **Critical Success** adds a serious effect (disarmed, pinned, exposed), and in a dangerous fight a margin of **+4 or more** advances Harm two tiers.
- **Armor and cover** do not add Harm buffer: they raise the bar to hit you (+1 die on your opposed roll).
- **Groups.** A group of minor opponents is one **Threat** with its own track: Holding, Breaking, Routed. A Success moves it down one stage, a Critical Success two.
- **Concede or retreat.** You can **concede** before an Exchange resolves: the fight ends for you on terms the GM sets (worse than winning, but you survive and take no more Harm). Or you can try to **retreat** with an opposed roll; failing costs another Exchange.
- **Combat and the Self.** Real Harm to another person raises your Stress by 1. Taking Wounded or worse is a Trauma moment if it hits a Boundary. E.G.O. dice can be spent on any roll in a fight, and a Hail Mary can happen mid-Exchange.

### Social conflict

It uses the same engine: your pool against the other side's, opposed when someone resists. **Preparation pays off:** an Investigation, Streetwise or Corporate Knowledge roll made ahead of time can give one bonus die on the next related roll (a bluff built on Deception gives the die too, but risks a Complication if tested). **Declare stakes first.** The four common flavors are **Negotiation** (Presence or Mind + Persuasion), **Pressure** (Presence + Persuasion, or Empathy used coldly), **Deception** (Presence + Deception) and **Commerce**.

### Gear, money and augments

- **Resources** (0 to 5) are your money; you start with 2. A Contract pays Resources equal to its Risk (one less if it came through an Office and was Risk 3 or 4). Each downtime phase costs 1. Prices: **1** for armor, a Light weapon or a medical kit; **2** for a Heavy weapon, attuned gear or a Backstreets augment; **3** for Fine attuned gear or a Nest augment. If you cannot pay, you may **borrow the shortfall** as a Debt Bond to the seller.
- **Weapons** are broad: **Improvised** (no bonus), **Light** (knives, sidearms), **Heavy** (+1 die on the attack, but a Complication risk where it does not belong), **Abnormality-touched** (case by case, usually a Trauma or Stress risk).
- **Attuned gear** carries one Sin and a Boon: **Weapon** +1 die on attacks tagged with its Sin; **Suit** +1 die on defense tagged with its Sin; **Tool** +1 die on a tagged roll with that Sin's Favored Skills; **Charm** ignores a Sin's Complication once per scene. You are **attuned** with rating 2 or more in its Sin; with 0 or 1 you can **borrow** it. The price is **Pull** (the first 1 each scene brings that Sin's Complication), **Tally** marks and **Wear**: each Pull adds a Wear, and at 3 Wear the gear is **Spent** until you pay 1 Resources and a downtime action.
- **Augmentations** add +1 die to one Skill. A **Nest** one costs 3 and is clean; a **Backstreets** one costs 2, brings a Complication on the first 1 each scene, and a Debt Bond of 1 to whoever fitted it.

### Between Contracts and growth

- **Downtime.** E.G.O. recovers (up to 2), Stress may drop once, Harm heals, you pay 1 Resources, you repair gear, and you can care for a Bond.
- **Marks.** At the end of each Contract you earn **1 Mark** (+1 if it was Risk 3 or 4, +1 once per session for a played Reckoning or a scene that grew a Bond). Spend them in downtime, in a scene that shows the training: a **Skill** +1 costs the **new rating** (cap 3), an **Attribute** +1 costs **three times the new rating** (cap 4). Raising Resolve raises your E.G.O. maximum by 1.
- **Grade.** Grade is what the City thinks of you. It rises through Contracts, Standing and proving moments, and an honest **Report** counts toward promotion (a falsified one counts double but opens a Heat Clock). It unlocks one dot at four points: **Grade 7** a Skill to 4, **Grade 5** an Attribute to 5, **Grade 3** a Skill to 5, **Grade 1** Risk 4 becomes normal work.

## 8. Create your character in 14 steps

Build in this order; each step leans on the last. The app's **New character** wizard follows the same order.

| # | Step | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Concept** | One line mixing who you are and what you do: *"A Backstreets medic who used to work Wing security."* |
| 2 | **Identity** | A short phrase about how you see yourself, not a class: *"The one who fixes people, not machines."* |
| 3 | **Background** | Where you come from. It can give one free Skill point, if your GM agrees. |
| 4 | **Occupation** | A package of three Skills at 2 (for example, medic: Medicine, Streetwise, Empathy). |
| 5 | **Affiliation** | Who you work for: an Office, a Wing, a Syndicate, an Association, a mutual-aid network, or nobody. Gives a starting Bond (Strength 1). |
| 6 | **Relationship** | Someone who matters to you. It can be **another player's character**. Gives a Bond (Strength 2). |
| 7 | **Principle** | A belief that could bend under real pressure. |
| 8 | **Your Wound** | The heart of the character; see below. |
| 9 | **Desire** | Something concrete you could chase this week. |
| 10 | **Vice** | A specific recurring weakness: *"can't say no to a patient"*, not "greedy". |
| 11 | **Ambition** | The question your whole story answers. |
| 12 | **Attributes** | Body, Mind, Presence and Resolve all start at 1. Spend **6 points**, no more than **4** in any one. |
| 13 | **Skills** | The twelve Skills start at 0. Your Occupation gives three at 2; spend **7 more points**, no more than **3** in any one. |
| 14 | **The rest** | **E.G.O. maximum = Resolve**, Stress 1, Resources 2, one piece of gear (Cost 2 or less), and 6 points of **Resonance** in the Sins. |

**Skills:** Combat, Athletics, Stealth, Investigation, Technology, Medicine, Persuasion, Deception, Empathy, Streetwise, Corporate Knowledge, Fixer Knowledge. A Skill is not locked to one Attribute: the GM names the pairing per roll from what you describe (Empathy + Mind to read someone clinically, Empathy + Resolve to sit with someone's grief).

### Your Wound, your Sin, your Vice

**The Wound** is one event seen three ways. Answer the three questions together, from the same moment:

1. **What happened to you?** This is your **Burden**: a concrete event in your past, written so it could trigger again. It becomes your first **Trauma**.
2. **What are you afraid of because of it?** This is your **Fear**. Make it specific: not "dying", but "dying without anyone knowing what happened to me".
3. **What line will you never cross again because of it?** This is your **Boundary**. Make it personal: not "I won't hurt children", but "I won't let someone die on my table because I hesitated".

The table needs a trigger, **not the worst version**. Share as much of the Burden as you want; nobody will press you for more.

**Your Sin.** Then ask: *how does that Wound feel?* Spend **6 points** of Resonance in the seven Sins (at most 3 in any one, and at least one at 2 or more). Common splits are 3/2/1, 3/3 or 2/2/2. Your highest Sin is your **Alignment**. It should visibly fit your Wound and your Vice.

| Sin | The Wound feels like... |
|---|---|
| **Wrath** | Rage at what happened, or at whoever let it happen. |
| **Lust** | Longing and giving: spreading a piece of yourself and holding on to whoever takes it. |
| **Sloth** | Numbness, hesitation, the freeze, going along with what others want. |
| **Gluttony** | Hunger. Never enough. |
| **Gloom** | Grief, guilt, sorrow carried. |
| **Pride** | Dignity and perfection: the need to be right, the machine that does not fail. |
| **Envy** | Comparison, inadequacy, wanting what someone else has. |

**A worked example.** Mira Sato, a Backstreets medic who used to work Wing security. *Identity:* "the one who fixes people, not machines". *Vice:* can't say no to a patient. *Boundary:* "I won't let someone die on my table because I hesitated". *Burden:* the night her hands went still and a patient she could have saved bled out. Her Wound feels like grief and guilt, so her Resonance goes mostly into **Gloom**.

## 9. One-page reference

**Roll.** Attribute + Skill = d10s. 7+ is a Success. Difficulty 1 to 3 (4+ exceptional). Critical Success at Difficulty + 2; Success at Difficulty; **Partial at Difficulty − 1**; Failure at −2 or worse; **0 Successes with a 1 = Critical Failure**.

**E.G.O.** 1 point = 1 extra die; a 1 or 2 on those dice = Complication. **Unsteady** under half: first 1 each scene is a Flashpoint. **Empty** at 0: every roll with a 1. Recovery: +1 night, +1 downtime scene, max +2 between Contracts. Refunds (Vice, techniques, gear) max 2 per scene.

**Stress.** 0 to 5. +1 Trauma trigger, Boundary crossed, Critical Failure, lost Bond, Manifestation. +2 Distortion. Lowers by 1 once between Contracts; a Reckoning lowers it. Stress 2 to 3: E.G.O. max −1. 4 to 5: −2 and Fraying.

**Harm.** Hurt (no penalty) → Injured (−1 die) → Wounded (−2 dice) → Maimed/Dying (out). A hit moves it one tier; margin +4 in a dangerous fight, two.

**Bond (Strength 1 to 3).** Spend 1: +1 die, Partial to Success (once per scene), or resist 1 Stress. It does not refill by itself.

**Vice.** The GM invokes it: you regain 1 E.G.O. (once per scene). **Trauma** trigger: Stress +1 and narrate a reaction.

**Hail Mary.** E.G.O. would hit 0 at a Boundary moment (or dig deep: all E.G.O., at least 2, +1 Difficulty). Resolve + Skill, Difficulty 2 to 3, −1 die per broken Boundary, spend a Bond for +1. Crit: clean Manifestation. Success: Manifestation at a cost. Partial: Partial Distortion. Failure: Complete Distortion.

**Sins.** Tag with rating 2 or more. Wheel (clockwise): Sloth, Wrath, Pride, Envy, Gluttony, Lust, Gloom. Strong against the next two, weak against the previous two: +1 or −1 die. Under or Riding: Fit +1 / −2. Total never above +3 or below −3.

**Fights.** Exchanges, no Initiative. Attack: Body or Mind + Combat vs Body + Athletics or Combat. Armor and cover: +1 die for the defender. You may concede.

**Growth.** Marks per Contract. Skill +1 costs the new rating (cap 3). Attribute +1 costs 3 × the new rating (cap 4).

## 10. Before you sit down

- [ ] I can say in one line **who my character works for** and what they keep from each job.
- [ ] I have written my **Burden, Fear and Boundary** from one moment, and I am comfortable sharing the Fear and the Boundary aloud.
- [ ] I know my **Vice** and my **Desire**.
- [ ] I have **two Bonds**, one of them with someone at the table if possible.
- [ ] Attributes add to **10** (4 maximum each); Skills add to **13** (3 maximum each, 2 from the package); Resonance adds to **6**.
- [ ] I know where my character **sleeps**, and whether it is a residential building.
- [ ] I have **one thing I do not want to see** in the game, to tell the GM.

**At the table:** say what your character does and let the GM set the Difficulty; ask what a Success, a Partial and a Failure would cost before you roll; and use the **?** buttons in the app to open the manual at the right section any time you are unsure.
