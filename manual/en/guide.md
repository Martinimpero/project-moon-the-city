# THE CITY: A PLAYER'S QUICK GUIDE

*Ten minutes of reading before your first session: where you live, who you answer to, what the City is like for each kind of person, and how to build your character. Everything here is a starting point; your GM may swap any name for one of their own.*

## 1. Where you are

The City is enormous, old and layered: a corporate tower can stand two streets from a district the utilities forgot. Nobody sees all of it. It is split into Districts, and each one belongs to a **Wing** (a corporation). Every District has two faces: the **Nest**, its walled and protected corporate core, and the **Backstreets** around it, where the Wing rarely bothers to manage anything directly. Beyond the walls are the **Outskirts**, where the Head sends whatever it will not have inside. (The **City** button in the app shows the map of the Districts.)

**When you play.** The game starts just after the **White Nights and the Dark Days**: seven days when the City lit up and then went dark. Since then people have begun to **Distort**, and a few have answered with **E.G.O.** Both are recent, rare and badly understood. Most Cityfolk have never seen either, and nobody trusts what they are told.

**Numbness.** Loss is common here, so people rarely hold funerals, keep their distance and avoid strong ties. Fixers die young and know it. That is why a **Bond** matters more here than it would anywhere else, and why a scene where someone lets another person get close should be allowed to land.

## 2. Who runs things

| Power | What it is | How it presses on you |
|---|---|---|
| **The Head** | Sits above everything and is never seen. It sets the Taboos and steps in only when one is broken. | Like weather: a file that goes missing, an order that overrides a sensible decision. It does not negotiate or explain. |
| **Wings** | Corporations built around a **Singularity**, a contained piece of something not fully of this world. Each holds a District. | Through **Employment**: a Bond that cuts both ways. They pay well and protect their own, which makes it hard to spend a Bond against them. |
| **Syndicates** | They run the Backstreets day to day, on favors, debt and territory. The five biggest are the **Five Fingers**. | Through **Debt** and **Obligation**: Bonds owed to people who collect without much restraint. |
| **Offices** | Small business outfits where most working Fixers actually punch in. | Through the daily grind: quotas, a cut off every payment, and whether the person who hands out work still trusts you. |
| **Associations** | Twelve formal bodies that license and grade Fixers. The largest and most trusted is the **Hana Association**. | Through the licence and the **Report** you file after every job. Standing lost with one follows you everywhere. |

## 3. How people live

**In a Nest.** Safe, well paid and watched. The Wing protects its people with real medical care and real counsellors, and asks for a loyalty that looks a lot like family right up until it doesn't. Nests are made of **feathers**: the people the Wing protects, taxes and may evict. Every Singularity runs on somebody's suffering, and the staff may or may not know whose.

**In the Backstreets.** Market stalls under damp scaffolding, deals struck on credit, and a Syndicate that "lends" to anyone who looks like they are struggling and always collects. A **migration permit** from the Backstreets to a Nest is a dream prize, and also a leash: the Wing can take it back. The Backstreets are also where the Night is worst.

**As a Fixer.** You take jobs nobody official wants to sign: recovery, negotiation, investigation, protection, removal. Most Fixers work through an **Office**, which keeps a cut of every payment (the Ninth Registry Office keeps 30%, by a formula nobody has seen written). An *Associate* Office cannot refuse the work it is given. Independents are free, rare and rarely last. You carry a **Grade** from 9 (least proven) to 1, earned only by jobs that were actually done and vouched for. Most jobs are **Contracts** with a Risk from 1 to 4, and by the time one reaches you the client's name is usually the least true thing about it.

**Everyday life.**
- **Money** is Ahn. Most people spend nearly all of it on living costs and taxes. In the game it is your **Resources** (0 to 5).
- **Augments** (prosthetics and upgrades) are almost universal among Fixers. Nest augments are good and expensive; Backstreets augments are cheap and have side effects. Only the brain stays organic. An augment is also a debt to whoever made it.
- **Workshops** make gear and run on patents. The best ones choose their clients, and a no-name Grade 9 may be refused.
- **The Outskirts** are where the Head dumps what it will not have inside. People nobody wants end up there.

## 4. The Night and the Taboos

**The Night.** Every night, for about eighty minutes (roughly 3:13 to 4:34), the Backstreets lose almost all law. Two rules hold: **residential buildings are not touched**, and **nothing may be recorded** (only eyewitness testimony counts, and reporting what happened draws the attention of the Claw). The Head's **Sweepers** come through in three waves and consume everything left outdoors. Criminals use the Night to dispose of people. Know where your character sleeps, and where they are at 3 a.m.

**The Taboos.** The Head's laws are few and absolute. Examples: human-like machines and AI, resurrection, cloning beyond seven days, limits on firearms, tax evasion, and breaking the rules of the Night. A Wing may add its own **house Taboos** inside its District. Breaking one brings a **Taboo Hunter**: a Fixer hired by a Wing to stop the infraction and recover the evidence, with lethal force allowed.

## 5. Words you will hear

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Fixer** (Agent) | Licensed freelancer. Most player characters are one. |
| **Grade** | Your proven standing, 9 (new) to 1 (among the most capable). |
| **Contract / Risk** | A job with a client, an objective, a payment and a danger rating from 1 to 4. |
| **Bond** | A tie to another person: Trust, Fear, Debt, Affection, Obligation or Hatred, with a Strength of 1 to 3. You can spend it. |
| **E.G.O.** | What you have left inside. You spend it for extra dice. When it runs low, you get shaky. |
| **Stress** | What the City costs you (0 to 5). It lowers your E.G.O. maximum. |
| **Distortion** | A person's self giving way to something monstrous. Recent, rare, feared. |
| **Abnormality** | Something that does not follow the City's rules. Solved by investigation and care before violence. |
| **Singularity** | The contained piece of something strange at the heart of a Wing. |
| **Clock** | A circle of 4, 6 or 8 segments that fills as pressure builds. |
| **Heat** | The attention of an institution on you. A Clock that, once full, sends someone to ask questions. |

## 6. The game in a minute

- **The roll.** Add one Attribute and one Skill: that many d10. Every **7, 8, 9 or 10** is a Success. Your GM says the **Difficulty** (usually 1 to 3) before you roll.
- **Reading it.** At or above the Difficulty you succeed; above it by 2, a Critical Success. One short is a **Partial** ("you do it, but..." or "you fail, and..."). Two short is a **Failure**. Zero Successes with a 1 showing is a **Critical Failure**.
- **Spend what you have.** 1 E.G.O. is 1 extra die (a 1 or 2 on those dice is a Complication). A Bond can add a die or upgrade a Partial to a Success.
- **Sins.** Your **Resonance** in the seven Sins lets you **tag** a roll with one you have at 2 or more. Each Sin is strong against two and weak against two (the **Wheel**). A Sin you rate **0** cannot be tagged.
- **Harm.** There are no hit points: Hurt, Injured, Wounded, then out of the scene.
- **The Hail Mary.** When your E.G.O. would drop to 0 at a moment that touches your Boundary, you make a last stand: roll Resolve + a Skill. A good result is an **E.G.O. Manifestation**, a bad one a **Distortion**, and your Stress rises either way.

## 7. Build your character in 14 steps

Build in this order; each step leans on the last. The app's **New character** wizard follows the same order.

| # | Step | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Concept** | One line mixing who you are and what you do: *"A Backstreets medic who used to work Wing security."* |
| 2 | **Identity** | A short phrase about how you see yourself, not a class: *"The one who fixes people, not machines."* |
| 3 | **Background** | Where you come from. It can give one free Skill point, if your GM agrees. |
| 4 | **Occupation** | A package of three Skills at 2 (for example, medic: Medicine, Streetwise, Empathy). |
| 5 | **Affiliation** | Who you work for: an Office, a Wing, a Syndicate, an Association, a mutual-aid network, or nobody. Gives a starting Bond (Strength 1). |
| 6 | **Relationship** | Someone who matters to you. It can be **another player's character**. Gives a Bond (Strength 2). |
| 7 | **Principle** | A belief that could bend under real pressure. |
| 8 | **Your Wound** | The heart of the character; see section 8. |
| 9 | **Desire** | Something concrete you could chase this week. |
| 10 | **Vice** | A specific recurring weakness: *"can't say no to a patient"*, not "greedy". |
| 11 | **Ambition** | The question your whole story answers. |
| 12 | **Attributes** | Body, Mind, Presence and Resolve all start at 1. Spend **6 points**, no more than **4** in any one. |
| 13 | **Skills** | The twelve Skills start at 0. Your Occupation gives three at 2; spend **7 more points**, no more than **3** in any one. |
| 14 | **The rest** | **E.G.O. maximum = Resolve**, Stress 1, Resources 2, one piece of gear (Cost 2 or less), and 6 points of **Resonance** in the Sins. |

**Skills:** Combat, Athletics, Stealth, Investigation, Technology, Medicine, Persuasion, Deception, Empathy, Streetwise, Corporate Knowledge, Fixer Knowledge.

## 8. Your Wound, your Sin, your Vice

**The Wound** is one event seen three ways. Answer the three questions together, from the same moment:

1. **What happened to you?** This is your **Burden**: a concrete event in your past, written so it could trigger again. It becomes your first **Trauma**.
2. **What are you afraid of because of it?** This is your **Fear**. Make it specific: not "dying", but "dying without anyone knowing what happened to me".
3. **What line will you never cross again because of it?** This is your **Boundary**. Make it personal: not "I won't hurt children", but "I won't let someone die on my table because I hesitated".

The table needs a trigger, **not the worst version**. Share as much of the Burden as you want; nobody will press you for more.

**Your Sin.** Then ask: *how does that Wound feel?* Spend **6 points** of Resonance in the seven Sins (at most 3 in any one, and at least one at 2 or more). Common splits are 3/2/1, 3/3 or 2/2/2. Your highest Sin is your **Alignment**. It should visibly fit your Wound and your Vice.

| Sin | The Wound feels like... |
|---|---|
| **Wrath** | Rage at what happened, or at whoever let it happen. |
| **Lust** | Longing and giving: spreading a piece of yourself and holding on to whoever takes it. |
| **Sloth** | Numbness, hesitation, the freeze, going along with what others want. |
| **Gluttony** | Hunger. Never enough. |
| **Gloom** | Grief, guilt, sorrow carried. |
| **Pride** | Dignity and perfection: the need to be right, the machine that does not fail. |
| **Envy** | Comparison, inadequacy, wanting what someone else has. |

**A worked example.** Mira Sato, a Backstreets medic who used to work Wing security. *Identity:* "the one who fixes people, not machines". *Vice:* can't say no to a patient. *Boundary:* "I won't let someone die on my table because I hesitated". *Burden:* the night her hands went still and a patient she could have saved bled out. Her Wound feels like grief and guilt, so her Resonance goes mostly into **Gloom**.

## 9. Before you sit down

- [ ] I can say in one line **who my character works for** and what they keep from each job.
- [ ] I have written my **Burden, Fear and Boundary** from one moment, and I am comfortable sharing the Fear and the Boundary aloud.
- [ ] I know my **Vice** and my **Desire**.
- [ ] I have **two Bonds**, one of them with someone at the table if possible.
- [ ] Attributes add to **10** (4 maximum each); Skills add to **13** (3 maximum each, 2 from the package); Resonance adds to **6**.
- [ ] I know where my character **sleeps**, and whether it is a residential building.
- [ ] I have **one thing I do not want to see** in the game, to tell the GM.

**At the table:** say what your character does and let the GM set the Difficulty; ask what a Success, a Partial and a Failure would cost before you roll; and use the **?** buttons in the app to open the manual at the right section any time you are unsure.
