## B. Quick Reference: The Core Roll

Pool = Attribute + Skill (or Attribute alone if untrained). Roll that many d10s. A 7, 8, 9, or 10 is a Success.

| Difficulty | What it represents |
|---|---|
| 1 | Routine, but not risk-free |
| 2 | Genuinely challenging, professional-grade |
| 3 | The City actively pushing back |
| 4+ | Exceptional — needs prep, Help, or E.G.O. |

| Result | Successes vs. Difficulty |
|---|---|
| Critical Success | ≥ Difficulty + 2 |
| Success | ≥ Difficulty |
| Partial | = Difficulty − 1 |
| Failure | ≤ Difficulty − 2 |
| Critical Failure (check first) | 0 Successes, at least one die shows 1 (E.G.O. dice count); overrides Partial and Failure |

Opposed rolls: compare Successes from the side trying to change things: margin +3 or more Critical Success, +1 or +2 Success, 0 or −1 Partial, −2 or worse Failure. Ties favor the defender.

At Difficulty 1 there is no Failure band; the worst ordinary result is a Partial.

**What a 1 does:**

| Die showing 1 | Effect |
|---|---|
| Any die, with zero Successes in the roll | Critical Failure |
| Any die, while Unsteady | the first 1 each scene triggers a Flashpoint |
| Any die, while Empty | every roll showing a 1 triggers a Flashpoint (one per roll) |
| An E.G.O. die showing 1 or 2 | a Complication |

## C. Quick Reference: E.G.O., Stress, and Harm

**Spending E.G.O.:** 1 E.G.O. = 1 extra die. Any die showing 1–2 = one Complication.

**Recovering E.G.O.:** +1 per full night's rest, +1 more per genuine downtime scene. Maximum +2 between Contracts.

**Running low:** below half E.G.O. maximum (judged at the start of the roll, before spending), a character is Unsteady — the first 1 rolled each scene triggers a Flashpoint (the character's Vice takes over for a beat). At 0 E.G.O., a character is Empty — every roll that shows a 1 triggers a Flashpoint (one per roll). A maximum of 2 or less skips Unsteady.

**What raises Stress:** +1 for a Trauma trigger, a Boundary violation, a Critical Failure, a Bond lost outright, or an E.G.O. Manifestation. +2 for a Distortion. Stress only comes down through downtime tied to a Bond (−1, at most once between Contracts) or a Reckoning. At Stress 2+, a forced E.G.O. loss (interrogation, psychological attack, a bad Abnormality encounter) takes 1 extra point; at Stress 4+, 2 extra.

**Stress → E.G.O. max:**

| Stress | E.G.O. max modifier |
|---|---|
| 0–1 | none |
| 2–3 | −1 |
| 4–5 | −2, and Fraying (+1 Difficulty on Hail Mary) |

E.G.O. maximum never drops below 1.

**Harm track:** Hurt (no penalty) → Injured (−1 die) → Wounded (−2 die, needs treatment) → Maimed/Dying (out of the scene).

**The Hail Mary:** triggers when E.G.O. would hit 0 at a Boundary-level moment, or when a player digs deep (spends all remaining E.G.O., at least 2, and +1 Difficulty; not on a line with an unhealed Scar). A Success needs an **Acceptance** (the character states what they accept about themselves) or it becomes a Partial Distortion. Resolve + relevant Skill, Difficulty 2–3. −1 die per Boundary already broken this campaign; may spend a Bond for +1 die.

| Result | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Critical Success | E.G.O. Manifestation, clean. Stress +1. |
| Success | E.G.O. Manifestation, at a cost. Stress +1. |
| Partial | Partial Distortion — genuinely transformed and monstrous, but the character keeps the wheel. Stress +2. |
| Failure | Complete Distortion — the self is pushed out; lasts until the fiction brings them back. Stress +2. |
| Critical Failure | Complete Distortion, already severe — death or permanence are live options from the start. Stress +2. |

## G. Quick Reference: The Sins

**Resonance:** 6 points at creation, max 3; highest Sin is Alignment. Rating 1 is a flicker; rating 2 or more does things. Signature Technique at 2+.

**The wheel** (each Sin is strong against the next two, weak against the two before; order: Sloth, Wrath, Pride, Envy, Gluttony, Lust, Gloom):

| Sin | Strong against | Weak against |
|---|---|---|
| Sloth | Wrath, Pride | Gloom, Lust |
| Wrath | Pride, Envy | Sloth, Gloom |
| Pride | Envy, Gluttony | Wrath, Sloth |
| Envy | Gluttony, Lust | Pride, Wrath |
| Gluttony | Lust, Gloom | Envy, Pride |
| Lust | Gloom, Sloth | Gluttony, Envy |
| Gloom | Sloth, Wrath | Lust, Gluttony |

**Swing:** Strong +1 die, Weak −1 die (±2 at rating 4, ±3 at rating 5). Needs rating 2+. Matchup plus Fit never beyond ±3.

**Fit (only while Under a Sin):** Favored +1, Hindered −2 (+2 / −3 at rating 4+).

| Sin | Favored | Hindered |
|---|---|---|
| Wrath | Combat, Athletics, Persuasion | Stealth, Investigation, Medicine |
| Lust | Persuasion, Empathy, Deception | Investigation, Technology, Corporate Knowledge |
| Sloth | Stealth, Medicine, Investigation | Athletics, Combat, Streetwise |
| Gluttony | Investigation, Streetwise, Corporate Knowledge | Stealth, Empathy, Medicine |
| Gloom | Empathy, Medicine, Investigation | Persuasion, Deception, Athletics |
| Pride | Persuasion, Corporate Knowledge, Fixer Knowledge | Empathy, Stealth, Deception |
| Envy | Deception, Stealth, Technology | Persuasion, Medicine, Fixer Knowledge |

**Under a Sin:** Riding it (free, one per scene); its Urge took your Flashpoint; Distorted; Overtaken (4) and Unsteady or Empty; Saturated (5).

**Drift:** every fourth session, a Sin leading by 8 or more tags gains +1 (max 4); the least-tagged Sin loses 1. **Saturated (5):** always Under, must tag each scene or +1 Stress.

**With the Self:** E.G.O. Complication on a tagged roll is the Sin's; refunds capped at 2 E.G.O. per scene; Permanent E.G.O. counts as rating 4; Distorted characters are Exposed (+1 die against them from any Sin that beats theirs).

## H. Quick Reference: Resources and Attuned Gear

**Resources:** 0 to 5, start 2. A Contract pays Resources equal to its Risk (minus 1 for the Office's cut at Risk 3 or 4). Each downtime phase costs 1. Cost: 1 armor, Light weapon, kit; 2 Heavy weapon, attuned gear, Backstreets augmentation; 3 Fine attuned gear, Nest augmentation; 4 Workshop extraction. Short of money: a Debt Bond (up to 3).

**Attuned gear:** Weapon +1 die on attacks tagged with its Sin; Suit +1 die on defense tagged with its Sin; Tool +1 die on a tagged roll using a Favored Skill of the Sin; Charm ignores the Sin's Complication once per scene. Fine (Cost 3): +2 if attuned at rating 3+.

**Attuned** = your rating in its Sin is 2+. **Borrowed** = rating 0 or 1: tag as if rating 2 (swing ±1), no Fit, no Riding, no technique.

**Price:** Pull (the Sin's Complication on the first 1 each scene; every 1 if borrowing, Under, or Overtaken); Tally (a mark for the Sin; two if borrowing); Wear (1 per Pull; Spent at 3, repair costs 1 Resources and a downtime action).

**Limits:** one Boon per roll; matchup + Fit + Boon never beyond +4; two pieces at once (three or more: all Pull on every 1).

## K. Glossary

Terms used in this book, with where to read more. Part numbers are shown as Part and section.

**A**

- **Abnormality** (Part VII §6): Something that doesn't follow the City's rules: contained, studied, sometimes loose. Run it from behavior and consequence, not a stat block.
- **Acceptance** (Part IV §4): The player's answer to the Verdict: they deny it and say who the character is underneath. Without it a Success becomes a Partial Distortion.
- **Alignment** (Part X §2): A character's, NPC's or Abnormality's highest Sin. It is what an opposing tag is compared to when no tag is made.
- **Ambition** (Part III §7): The question the character's whole story should eventually answer.
- **Approach** (Part VII §6): One of four ways to deal with an Abnormality: Instinct, Insight, Attachment, or Repression.
- **Arbiter** (Part VII §7): An agent of the Head, called only for the worst threats to the City. Never a PC's opponent in a roll.
- **Asset** (Part V §7): A thing the crew owns together and buys from the Fund: Safehouse, Getaway, Workshop contact, Informant, Clinic, Cover.
- **Association** (Part I §2, VII §3): One of the City's twelve formal bodies. It licenses and grades Fixers, sorts work and enforces contracts. The Hana Association is the largest.
- **Attuned** (Part VI §5): Having rating 2 or more in a gear's Sin. An attuned wearer tags it as normal.
- **Attuned gear** (Part VI §5): Equipment keyed to one Sin, with a Boon and the drawbacks Pull, Tally and Wear.
**B**

- **Backstreets** (Part I §1, VII §2): The ground around a Wing's Nest, run day to day by Syndicates. Bound by the same Taboos as the Nest.
- **Beholder** (Part VII §7): An agent of the Eye: it doesn't punish, it notices. Being noticed is the warning.
- **Bond** (Part V §1): A tie to another person, with a Type (Trust, Fear, Debt, Affection, Obligation, Hatred) and a Strength of 1 to 3. Spent for dice, upgrades, or Stress relief.
- **Boon** (Part VI §5): The bonus a piece of attuned gear gives on a roll tagged with its Sin.
- **Borrowed** (Part VI §5): Using attuned gear with rating 0 or 1 in its Sin: you tag it as if rating 2, with no Fit, Riding or technique, and a heavier price.
- **Boundary** (Part III §3, IV §4): The one line a character will never cross again because of their Wound. The fault line the Hail Mary keys off.
- **Breach Clock** (Part VII §6): A Clock of how close an Abnormality is to breaking containment. When it fills, the Breach Effect happens.
- **Burden** (Part III §3): The concrete past event at the heart of a character's Wound. Written so it can trigger again; becomes the starting Trauma.
**C**

- **Charm** (Part VI §5): A kind of attuned gear. Once per scene it ignores the Sin's Complication from an E.G.O. die.
- **Claw** (Part VII §7): An agent of the Head's punishment: carries out sentences on anyone who breaks a Taboo, with no appeal.
- **Clock** (Part II §4): A circle of 4, 6 or 8 segments that tracks pressure over time. One segment per meaningful setback, never more than two.
- **Color** (Part IX §3): A personal title for a Grade 1 Fixer with a Permanent E.G.O. and a defining feat. Sent after Stars of the City.
- **Complication** (Part IV §1): A concrete cost the GM drops into the fiction: from an E.G.O. die showing 1 or 2, a Pull, or a Partial.
- **Concede** (Part VI §7): Ending a fight on the GM's survivable terms before an Exchange resolves.
- **Contract** (Part V §4): A job with a Client, Objective, Payment, Complication Clause, and a Risk rating from 1 to 4.
- **Crew Bond** (Part V §7): A Bond the crew holds on itself. Spent to add a die to a Helped roll. At 0 the crew is Fractured.
- **Critical Failure** (Part II §2): Zero Successes with at least one die showing a 1. Checked first; it overrides Partial and Failure.
- **Critical Success** (Part II §2): Successes at or above Difficulty + 2: the full effect plus something extra.
**D**

- **Difficulty** (Part II §1): The number of Successes a roll needs, set aloud before the dice. Usually 1 to 3.
- **Dig deep** (Part IV §4): Triggering the Hail Mary on purpose by spending all remaining E.G.O. (at least 2), at +1 Difficulty. Not on a Scarred line.
- **Distortion** (Part IV §6): A character surrendering to the crisis and becoming something monstrous. Partial: the player keeps the wheel. Complete: the Distortion drives.
- **Downtime phase** (Part VIII §10): The stretch between Contracts: two downtime actions per character, plus rest.
- **Drastic moment** (Part IV §5): The GM's declaration that the very thing a Permanent E.G.O. is built on is being broken. The E.G.O. can then be Distorted.
- **Dread** (Part VII §6): E.G.O. lost on first sight of an Abnormality whose Class outranks your Grade.
- **Drift** (Part X §6): Every fourth session, a Sin you tagged far more than any other gains a rating and your least-used Sin loses one.
**E**

- **E.G.O.** (Part IV §1): A pool, up to Resolve, of how much of themselves a character can draw on. Spent as extra dice; lost, it makes them unsteady.
- **Empty** (Part IV §1): At 0 E.G.O.: every roll showing a 1 triggers a Flashpoint (one per roll).
- **Exchange** (Part VI §3): A round of combat: everyone involved acts once, in an order set by the fiction.
- **Exposed** (Part X §5): A Distorted character is Exposed: any Sin that beats theirs gets +1 die against them.
- **Eye** (Part VII §7): One of the three entities at the top of the City. It watches through its Beholders.
**F**

- **Fallen** (Part IV §6): A permanent transformation after a Complete Distortion on a Scarred line. An Abnormality if the self remains, a Peccatulum if not.
- **Fear** (Part III §3): The specific, nameable thing a character is afraid of because of their Wound. The Verdict speaks in its words.
- **Feather** (Part VII §10): A person a Wing protects, taxes and may evict.
- **Fine gear** (Part VI §5): Attuned gear at Cost 3. Its Boon is +2 if you are attuned at rating 3 or more.
- **Fit** (Part X §4): While Under a Sin, its Favored Skills get +1 die and its Hindered Skills −2 dice.
- **Five Fingers** (Part VII §4): The five most powerful Syndicates, strong enough to rank as Stars of the City.
- **Fixer** (Part I §2): Someone who takes on work nobody official wants their name on. Licensed and graded by an Association.
- **Flashpoint** (Part IV §1): When Unsteady or Empty and a 1 shows, the character's Vice or Sin's Urge takes the wheel for a beat.
- **Fractured** (Part V §7): A crew whose Crew Bond is 0: no Help rolls between members until a scene with everyone present restores it.
- **Fraying** (Part IV §2): At Stress 4 or 5: E.G.O. maximum falls by 2 and every Hail Mary roll takes +1 Difficulty.
- **Fund** (Part V §7): The crew's shared Resources, 0 to 5, spent mainly on Assets.
**G**

- **Grade** (Part IX §1, §4): A number from 9 (least proven) to 1 (among the most capable) that an institution assigns. Also the Threat Grade of an opponent.
**H**

- **Hail Mary** (Part IV §4): The crisis when E.G.O. would hit 0 at a Boundary moment, or when a player digs deep. Roll Resolve + Skill; the result is a Manifestation or a Distortion.
- **Harm** (Part VI §2): Physical injury on a four-tier track: Hurt, Injured, Wounded, Maimed/Dying. No hit points.
- **Head** (Part I §1, VII §7): The entity that sets the City's Taboos and steps in only when one is broken. Never rolls against a PC.
- **Heat** (Part V §5): A faction's growing attention, tracked on a Clock. A full Heat Clock is a concrete institutional response.
- **Help** (Part II §3): Another character rolls at Difficulty 1; each Success adds a die to the lead roll. One helper per roll.
**I**

- **Identity** (Part III §3): A short self-concept phrase, not a class. The seed for Manifestation and Distortion.
- **Integration** (Part IV §7): A second, harder Reckoning over a Healed Scar: grants a Permanent E.G.O.
**L**

- **Ledger** (Part V §7): The crew's record of each Contract: client, Risk, Payment and Report.
- **Line / Veil** (Part VIII §11): A Line is something that never appears in the game. A Veil is something that happens off-screen.
**M**

- **Manifestation** (Part IV §5): A Hail Mary success: the character's identity made solid. Volatile (this scene) the first time; Permanent the second time on the same fault line.
- **Marks** (Part IX §7): Growth points earned per Contract and spent on Skills and Attributes.
**N**

- **Nest** (Part VII §2): A Wing's corporate core. Its District is the Nest plus the Backstreets around it.
- **Night, the** (Part VII §9): About eighty minutes each night when the Backstreets lose almost all law. Residential areas are untouched and nothing may be recorded; Sweepers come in three waves.
- **Notice Clock** (Part VII §7): A slow, campaign-length Clock the GM fills privately from actions that draw the Head's attention.
**O**

- **Office** (Part VII §5): A small business that takes Fixer contracts and passes work down. Answers to an Association.
- **Office Grade** (Part V §7): The best Grade among a crew's PCs. Sets the highest Risk the Office is offered.
- **Origin** (Part VII §6): Whose will, desire or fear an Abnormality carries.
- **Overtaken** (Part X §6): A Sin at rating 4: its Urge is always your Flashpoint, you are Under it when Unsteady or Empty, and your swing is ±2.
**P**

- **Partial** (Part II §2): Successes equal to Difficulty − 1: you succeed with a cost, or fail and it gets worse.
- **Peccatulum** (Part IV §6): A mindless thing that is only one Sin, left when a Distortion loses the self.
- **Permanent E.G.O.** (Part IV §5): The stable form of a Manifestation: a named ability with a physical form, costing 1 E.G.O. It closes its fault line to Distortion except in a drastic moment.
- **Pool** (Part II §1): Attribute + Skill: the number of d10 rolled. A 7 or higher is a Success.
- **Pull** (Part VI §5): Attuned gear provoking its Sin: the Sin's Complication on the first 1 of a scene (every 1 if borrowing, Under, or Overtaken).
**R**

- **Reach Clock** (Part IV §6): An optional Clock of real attempts to bring a Distorted character back.
- **Reckoning** (Part IV §7): A played scene in which a character confronts their wound. Lowers Stress and can Heal a Scar.
- **Renown** (Part IX): An independent's Grade: reputation alone, with no formal body.
- **Report** (Part VII §3): What a Fixer tells the Association after a Contract. Honest reports bank progress; falsified ones are faster but open a Heat Clock.
- **Repression** (Part VII §6): Suppressing an Abnormality by force. The last resort before a fight or a flight.
- **Resonance** (Part X §2): A character's rating, 0 to 4, in each of the seven Sins. Six points at creation.
- **Resources** (Part VI §5): Money as a single 0–5 rating. Contracts pay it, upkeep drains it, gear has a Cost in it.
- **Return, the** (Part X §7): What brings a Distorted Sin back: a scene that delivers it counts as recovery.
- **Riding** (Part X §4): Declaring a Sin for a whole scene: free, one per scene, tags every roll, and costs you its Hindered Skills.
- **Risk** (Part V §4): A Contract's rating from 1 to 4, mapped to Difficulty and to Resources paid.
**S**

- **Saturated** (Part X §6): A Sin at rating 5: always Under it, and you must tag it each scene or take +1 Stress.
- **Scar** (Part IV §6): What a Distortion leaves behind: a Tell and a Broken Boundary (−1 die). Healed by a Reckoning.
- **Sin** (Part X §1): One of seven shapes a Wound takes: Wrath, Lust, Sloth, Gluttony, Gloom, Pride, Envy.
- **Sin Tally** (Part X §6): A running count of the Sins you tag and ride, used for Drift.
- **Singularity** (Part I §1): A contained piece of something not fully of this world at the heart of a Wing's business.
- **Standing** (Part V §2): Your Bond Strength with an institution, summed: Unknown, Known, Trusted, Made. A narrative gate, not dice.
- **Star of the City** (Part IX §5): The Head's classification for the most dangerous threats, which it has chosen not to act on. Faced with several Clocks, not one roll.
- **Strained** (Part X §5): A Sin tied to a Scar: tagged rolls take −1 die and its technique is off until the Scar Heals.
- **Stress** (Part IV §2): A 0–5 track of how close a character's fault line is to breaking. Shrinks E.G.O. maximum; rarely comes down.
- **Sweepers** (Part VII §9): Head-sanctioned agents who consume everything outdoors during the Night, in three waves.
- **Syndicate** (Part I §1, VII §4): A power that runs the Backstreets on favors, debt and territory.
**T**

- **Taboo** (Part VII §7, §9): An absolute law set by the Head, or a house law set by a Wing for its own territory.
- **Taboo Hunter** (Part VII §9): A Fixer hired by a Wing to stop an infraction and recover the evidence.
- **Tag** (Part X §3): Naming a Sin on a roll and saying how the action expresses it. Needs rating 2+ (or gear).
- **Tell** (Part IV §6): The visible change a Scar leaves in how a character presents.
- **Threat** (Part VI §6): A group of minor opponents run as one track: Holding, Breaking, Routed.
- **Threat Grade** (Part IX §4): A Grade given to named opposition, mapped to Difficulty, dice rolled and Sin rating.
- **Trauma** (Part IV §3): A one-line trigger condition written when a Boundary or Fear is hit. When it recurs, Stress +1 and a narrated reaction.
**U**

- **Under (a Sin)** (Part X §4): Being driven by a Sin: Riding it, its Urge, Distortion, Overtaken, or Saturated. Fit applies only while Under.
- **Unsteady** (Part IV §1): Below half your E.G.O. maximum (judged at the start of a roll): the first 1 each scene triggers a Flashpoint.
- **Upkeep** (Part VIII §10): The 1 Resource a downtime phase costs, plus repairs and maintenance.
- **Urge** (Part X §7): A Sin's Flashpoint behavior: what it does when it takes the wheel.
**V**

- **Verdict, the** (Part IV §4): The voice of a character's Wound at the Hail Mary: what it has always said about them. The Acceptance is the answer.
- **Vice** (Part III §6): A specific, recurring weakness. The GM invokes it for a complication; the player regains 1 E.G.O.
- **Voice, the** (Part IV §1): The quiet voice only the character hears when pushed. At the Hail Mary it becomes the Verdict.
**W**

- **Wear** (Part VI §5): Marks on attuned gear from Pulls. At 3 the gear is Spent until repaired.
- **Wing** (Part I §1, VII §2): A corporation built around a Singularity. Holds a District: a Nest and its share of Backstreets.
- **Wing Support** (Part I §3, VI §2): What a Wing's Employment Bond grants: reduced Harm severity, a recovery bonus, stronger starting Bonds. It heals one extra Harm tier per downtime.
- **Wound** (Part III §3): A character's Burden, Fear and Boundary taken together as one thing.

## L. Pregenerated Characters

Four ready-to-play Fixers, built with the rules of Part III and Part X. They form one crew for a first Contract (the Session 01 kit in the vault): Wren, Dax, Lena and Tomas. Give each player one, or use them as examples when building your own. Each starts with Resources 2, Stress 1, no Harm and one piece of attuned gear (Part VI §5) of the Sin they are best in. The crew starts with a Crew Bond of 2 (Trust) and an empty Fund (Part V §7).

To build your own: copy the shape of a card, change the Wound and the Sins so they fit one another, and check the point-buys (Attributes 10 dots, Skills 13 dots, Resonance 6 points).

### Wren Okoro

*A Backstreets medic who used to work Wing security.*

- **Identity:** The one who fixes people, not machines. · **Occupation:** Backstreets Medic (unlicensed) · **Affiliation:** Mutual-aid network (a market street clinic) · **Background:** Raised in a Backstreets market
- **Attributes:** Body 2, Mind 2, Presence 2, Resolve 4 · **Skills:** Combat 1, Athletics 1, Stealth 1, Investigation 1, Technology 1, Medicine 3, Persuasion 1, Empathy 2, Streetwise 2
- **Wound.** Burden: A Wing guard bled out in her arms during a night shift after she waited for a supervisor's sign-off. Fear: Dying without anyone knowing what happened to me. Boundary: I won't let someone die on my table because I hesitated.
- **Vice:** Can't say no to a patient, even when it's obviously a trap. · **Desire:** Open a real clinic with a door that locks and a license on the wall. · **Principle:** Everyone gets one honest chance to be helped. · **Ambition:** Is a clean conscience something the City lets you keep?
- **E.G.O.** 4 / 4 · **Stress** 1 · **Grade** 9 · **Harm** none
- **Resonance:** Wrath 0 · Lust 1 · Sloth 2 · Gluttony 0 · Gloom 3 · Pride 0 · Envy 0 · **Alignment:** Gloom
- **Bonds:** Affection 2: Old Tabbi, who runs the market street clinic and took her in. Obligation 1: the mutual-aid network (she owes them shifts).
- **Resources** 2 · **Gear:** Rain Cape (Gloom Suit, attuned)
- **First hook:** A wounded vendor staggers into the market street and needs help right now.
