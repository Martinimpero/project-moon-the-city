# BEFORE YOU BUILD
### The City, in one page for players

Read this before you build a character. It gives you somewhere to be *from*. Everything here is a starting point: your GM may swap any name for one of their own.

## The City in four lines

The **Head** sets a few absolute laws (Taboos) and otherwise leaves the City alone. **Wings** are corporations built around a Singularity; each holds a **Nest** (its protected core) and the **Backstreets** around it. **Syndicates** run the Backstreets on debt and favors. **Offices** keep Fixers working, one quota and one cut at a time. You are probably a **Fixer**: licensed and graded by an **Association**, hired to do what nobody official wants their name on.

## When you play

The game starts just after the **White Nights and the Dark Days**: seven days when the City lit up and then went dark. Since then people have started to **Distort**, and a few have answered with **E.G.O.** Both are recent, rare and badly understood. Most Cityfolk have never seen either, and nobody trusts what they are told.

## Where you can be from (your Affiliation)

| You work for... | Example | What it is like |
|---|---|---|
| An **Office** (most Fixers) | Your GM names it: Offices are many, from small outfits to large dispatchers. | Quotas, and a cut off every payment before it reaches you. How big the cut is, and how clear the formula, is for your GM to say. |
| A **Wing** | One of the City's lettered corporations, one per District (see the **City** map in the app): for example **Nagel und Hammer** (N Corp.), **WARP Corp.** (W) or **TimeTrack Corporation** (T). **Lobotomy Corporation** (L) has fallen. | It pays well, protects you and has real counselors. That makes the Employment Bond hard to want to spend against it. Every Singularity runs on someone's suffering; your character may or may not know whose. |
| A **Syndicate** | The **Five Fingers**, the five great ones that hold the Backstreets: **Thumb** (strict hierarchy), **Index** (obeys written Prescripts), **Middle** (a family that takes Vengeance), **Ring** (art), **Pinky** (secret members, infiltration). Smaller Syndicates exist; some answer to a Finger. | It offers Debt to anyone who looks like they are struggling, and collects. |
| An **Association** | **The Hana Association**, the largest and most trusted of the City's twelve. | It licenses and grades you. You file a Report after every job. |
| **Nobody**, or a mutual-aid network | Independents are free, rare, and rarely last. | No salary and no cover, and nobody much trusts you. |

## A place to start: Threadmill Row

*(A Backstreets block invented for this game's example Contract; your GM may place it in any District, or use another.)* Stacked market stalls under permanently damp scaffolding; the smell of frying oil and machine coolant. Two Syndicate crews are fighting over the same stretch of black-market trade, and a recent knifing has the whole Row tense.

## The Night

Every night, for about eighty minutes (roughly 3:13 to 4:34), the Backstreets lose almost all law. **Residential buildings are safe. Nothing may be recorded.** The Head's **Sweepers** sweep the streets in three waves and consume whatever is outdoors. Know where your character sleeps, and where they are at 3 a.m.

## Everyday life

- **Money** is Ahn, and most people spend nearly all of it on living costs and taxes. In play it is your **Resources**.
- **Augmentation** is nearly universal among Fixers. Nest work is good and expensive; Backstreets work is cheap and has side effects. Only the brain stays organic.
- **Feathers** are the people a Wing protects, taxes and may evict. A **migration permit** from the Backstreets into a Nest is a dream prize, and a leash.
- **Numbness:** Cityfolk rarely hold funerals and avoid strong ties. That is why a Bond matters here, and why a scene where someone lets themselves be close should be allowed to land.

## Ideas for a Background

Each can grant one free Skill point, if the GM agrees, and a minor Bond.

| Background | Skill | Possible Bond |
|---|---|---|
| Backstreets market kid, raised between stalls | Streetwise | A stall-keeper who fed you (Trust or Debt) |
| Syndicate kid who left | Combat or Persuasion | The old crew boss (Debt or Fear) |
| Feather who lost a Nest permit | Corporate Knowledge | The Wing you worked for (Obligation or Hatred) |
| Raised by a Wing's clinic | Medicine | A nurse who covered for you (Trust) |
| An Office clerk's child | Investigation | A dispatcher who knows your name (Obligation) |
| Came back from the Outskirts | Athletics or Stealth | Someone who sent you out, or who took you in |
| Family member sentenced by the Claw | Deception | A relative you can't talk about (Fear) |
| Ex-augmentation test subject | Technology | The workshop that made you (Debt) |

## Desire and Ambition, with an example of each

- **Desire** is something you can chase this week: *clear the Debt I still owe my old crew without going back to them.*
- **Ambition** is the question your whole story answers: *can a man like me be trusted by anyone?*

## Five questions to bring to the table

1. Who do you work for, and what do they take off every job?
2. Where do you sleep, and is it residential?
3. Who would notice, and who would mourn, if you didn't come back?
4. What did the City do to you that you have never said aloud? (That is your Wound.)
5. Which Sin does that Wound feel like? (Part X, §1.)
