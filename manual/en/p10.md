# PART X — THE SINS

The City teaches every wound a shape. The Sins name those shapes. A Sin is not a vice and not a morality: it is the way a person's pain *moves* once it has to move, and it is how this game answers three questions the rest of the book leaves open. **What do I lean on when I'm pushed?** (your build). **Who do I beat, and who beats me?** (the wheel). **What do I become when I break?** (the form your E.G.O. and your Distortion take).

The Sins are a layer on top of everything in Parts II to VI. They do not replace the core roll, Bonds, Clocks, Harm, Stress, or the Hail Mary; they plug into them. Everything here is this game's own design (the wheel is not canon), and it is **untested at a real table**: §8 says which numbers to turn first.

## 1. The Seven Sins

| Sin | The Wound feels like... | Signature condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Wrath** | Rage at what happened, or at whoever let it happen: a fire that burns indiscriminately. | **Burn** |
| **Lust** | Longing and giving: the urge to spread a piece of yourself (a desire, a belief, what you know) and to hold on to whoever takes it. | **Bleed** |
| **Sloth** | Numbness, hesitation, the freeze, going along with what others want. | **Tremor** |
| **Gluttony** | Hunger. Never enough. | **Rupture** |
| **Gloom** | Grief, guilt, sorrow carried: sinking, and the deeper you go the harder it is to climb out. The one Sin with no deadly-sin twin. | **Sinking** |
| **Pride** | Dignity and perfection - the need to be right, to be the machine that doesn't fail. | **Poise** |
| **Envy** | Comparison - inadequacy, resentment of someone else's success, a static shock of wanting what they have. | **Charge** |

A Sin's *signature condition* is the status it leaves on a target. Techniques and the GM may apply it; each is described under the Sin in §7. Treat the conditions as optional until the table has played with Resonance for a few sessions.

## 2. Resonance

Every character has **Resonance**: a rating from 0 to 4 in each of the seven Sins. At character creation (right after The Wound, Part III step 8), ask: **what does your Wound feel like?** Then spend **6 points** of Resonance:

- Maximum **3** in any one Sin at creation, and at least one Sin at 2 or more. The usual spreads are 3/2/1, 3/3, or 2/2/2. Unspent points are lost.
- Your highest Sin is your **Alignment**. On a tie, choose and write it down.
- Rating **0**: you can't tag that Sin. Rating **1** is a *flicker*: you can tag it, but it gives no matchup and no Fit. Rating **2 or more** is when a Sin starts to do things.
- Your Alignment should visibly fit your Wound and your Vice. If it doesn't, revise one of them.
- Resonance is fixed at 6 points. Moving points is what Drift is for (§6).

**Signature Technique.** At rating 2 or more in a Sin you unlock that Sin's Signature Technique (§7), usable once per scene. More techniques (Minor, Major, Overdrive) live in the Compendium, outside this book.

**Breadth or depth.** 2/2/2 gives three Sins you can use for matchups. 3/3 gives two Sins with room to grow but fewer options in a fight. Neither is better; pick the one that fits the Wound.

## 3. Tagging and the Wheel

**Tagging.** You may **tag** any roll (a fight, an argument, an ordinary check) with one Sin you have rating 2 or more in, if the player can say how the action expresses that Sin ("I swing at him because I'm furious"; "I let the silence hang and say nothing"). The GM may veto a tag the fiction can't carry. A roll that uses attuned gear can be tagged with the gear's Sin (Part VI §5). You may tag a different Sin each roll, but only one tag per roll, and you never have to tag.

**What you're compared to.** Your tag is compared to the other side's tag, or, if they didn't tag, their **Alignment**. NPCs and Abnormalities have an Alignment and a **Sin rating from Grade**: Grade 9 to 5 is rating 2, Grade 4 to 2 is rating 3, Color or Grade 1 is rating 4. If the target is several people, the GM picks the most relevant one.

**The wheel.** The seven Sins sit in a circle. Each is **strong against the next two** and **weak against the two before it**; the other two are neutral.

Order, clockwise: **Sloth, Wrath, Pride, Envy, Gluttony, Lust, Gloom**, and back to Sloth.

| Sin | Strong against | Weak against | Neutral |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sloth | Wrath, Pride | Gloom, Lust | Envy, Gluttony |
| Wrath | Pride, Envy | Sloth, Gloom | Gluttony, Lust |
| Pride | Envy, Gluttony | Wrath, Sloth | Lust, Gloom |
| Envy | Gluttony, Lust | Pride, Wrath | Sloth, Gloom |
| Gluttony | Lust, Gloom | Envy, Pride | Sloth, Wrath |
| Lust | Gloom, Sloth | Gluttony, Envy | Wrath, Pride |
| Gloom | Sloth, Wrath | Lust, Gluttony | Pride, Envy |

| Matchup | Your pool |
|---|---|
| Strong | +1 die (+2 at rating 4, +3 at rating 5) |
| Weak | −1 die (−2 at rating 4, −3 at rating 5) |
| Neutral, same Sin, untagged, or rating 1 | no change |

Each side works out its own swing from the rating of the Sin it tagged, then both roll as usual. A roll never goes beyond **+3 or −3** from matchup and Fit (§4) combined.

> **EXAMPLE**
> Dax (Wrath 3) attacks Sable Rennick (Grade 4, Alignment Pride, so rating 3) with a Wrath-tagged swing. Wrath is strong against Pride, and Dax's rating is below 4, so the swing is +1: Body 4 + Combat 3 = 7, +1 = 8 dice. If Sable defends by tagging Pride (weak to Wrath, rating 3), she takes −1: Body 3 + Combat 2 = 5, −1 = 4 dice. If she doesn't tag, her pool stays at 5.

**Optional: the damage twist.** If you want matchups to hit harder than dice alone: on a **Strong** matchup, a Success advances Harm one extra tier; on a **Weak** matchup, a Success is treated as a Partial for Harm. Use it only if the table wants a swingier game.

## 4. Fit and Riding

A Sin doesn't only matter in a clash. Being **Under** a Sin colors every roll you make: it helps the Skills that suit it and hurts the ones that fight it. Wrath is good at force and bad at patience and care.

**Fit.** Each Sin has three **Favored** and three **Hindered** Skills. While you are Under a Sin with rating 2 or more:

| The Skill you roll is... | Your pool |
|---|---|
| Favored by that Sin | +1 die (+2 at rating 4 or more) |
| Hindered by that Sin | −2 dice (−3 at rating 4 or more) |
| Neither, or an Attribute-only roll | no change |

Fit goes by the **Skill** used, not the Attribute. A plain tag without being Under gives the matchup only, not Fit. Persuasion is Favored by Wrath only when you are applying *pressure*, not making a case.

| Sin | Favored (+1) | Hindered (−2) |
|---|---|---|
| Wrath | Combat, Athletics, Persuasion | Stealth, Investigation, Medicine |
| Lust | Persuasion, Empathy, Deception | Investigation, Technology, Corporate Knowledge |
| Sloth | Stealth, Medicine, Investigation | Athletics, Combat, Streetwise |
| Gluttony | Investigation, Streetwise, Corporate Knowledge | Stealth, Empathy, Medicine |
| Gloom | Empathy, Medicine, Investigation | Persuasion, Deception, Athletics |
| Pride | Persuasion, Corporate Knowledge, Fixer Knowledge | Empathy, Stealth, Deception |
| Envy | Deception, Stealth, Technology | Persuasion, Medicine, Fixer Knowledge |

**Under a Sin.** You are Under a Sin when:

1. You are **Riding** it (below).
2. Its **Urge** took your Flashpoint: Under for the rest of the scene.
3. You are **Distorted**: Under until you Return, with Fit at +2 / −3.
4. You are **Overtaken** (rating 4) and Unsteady or Empty: Under while that is true.
5. You are **Saturated** (rating 5): always Under.

You are Under one Sin at a time; if two apply, the GM names the one that is driving you. While you are Under a Sin you may tag any roll with it.

**Riding a Sin.** At the start of a scene (or the moment it starts to matter), you may declare that you are **Riding** a Sin you have rating 2 or more in, and say how ("I'm furious; I'm not holding back"). It is free and lasts until the scene ends, one Sin per scene. The price is the Hindered Skills, on every roll that scene, whether you meant to use them or not. While you ride, every roll counts as tagged with that Sin. The GM may refuse if the fiction won't carry it.

**Fit and matchup together.** Add them; the total is never above +3 or below −3.

> **EXAMPLE**
> Dax rides Wrath into the fight against Sable (Pride). Wrath is strong against Pride (+1) and Combat is Favored (+1): Body 4 + Combat 3 = 7, +2 = 9 dice. The same Dax, riding Wrath through a crowd, needs a quiet move: Stealth is Hindered. Body 4 + Stealth 1 = 5, −2 = 3 dice. Wren (Gloom 3) rides Gloom to treat a dying patient: Medicine is Favored. Mind 2 + Medicine 3 = 5, +1 = 6 dice. She wouldn't ride it to press a gang for a favor, because Persuasion is Hindered.

NPCs and Abnormalities don't use Fit: their pools are what the GM sets (Part IX §4).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Combat | + | . | − | . | . | . | . |
| Athletics | + | . | − | . | − | . | . |
| Stealth | − | . | + | − | . | − | + |
| Investigation | − | − | + | + | + | . | . |
| Technology | . | − | . | . | . | . | + |
| Medicine | − | . | + | − | + | . | − |

## 5. The Sins and the Self

Sins touch Part IV in six places.

- **Complications.** When you spend E.G.O. dice on a **Sin-tagged** roll, an E.G.O. die showing 1 or 2 triggers that Sin's **Complication** (§7) instead of a generic one. Untagged rolls use the ordinary Complications.
- **E.G.O. refunds are capped.** E.G.O. can return from the Vice, Sin techniques, E.G.O. gear, and Gifts. Combined, they restore at most **2 E.G.O. per scene**. Rest and downtime don't count toward the cap.
- **Flashpoint.** When a Flashpoint fires, it is your **Vice** or your Sin's **Urge** (§7): the Sin you tagged most recently, or your Alignment if none. The player chooses; if your rating in that Sin is 4 or more, it is the Urge. If it is the Urge, you are Under that Sin for the rest of the scene.
- **The Hail Mary.** The Sin of the crisis is the Sin your Boundary lives in: your Alignment, unless the Boundary is clearly about another Sin you have a rating in. The roll is **tagged** with that Sin; if the source of the crisis has an Alignment, the usual matchup applies. The Verdict (Part IV §4) is spoken in that Sin's voice.
- **Manifestation.** Your Manifestation carries the Sin; use its cues (§7) to answer "what does it look like?" A **Permanent E.G.O.** is Sin-typed: it always counts as tagged with that Sin, and counts as rating 4 for matchups. Its Signature Technique needs no rating.
- **Distortion.** The form comes from the Sin's Distortion entry (§7) plus the Wound and the Vice. While Distorted, you automatically tag your Sin on every roll and count as rating 4 for matchups; you are Under it until you Return. You are also **Exposed**: any Sin that beats yours gets +1 die against you. The Sin's **Return** (§7) is what the GM can use to judge whether a scene brings the character back: if a scene delivers it, treat the character as recovered. A **Scar** leaves the Sin of the broken Boundary **Strained** until the Scar Heals: tagged rolls with it take −1 die and you can't use its Signature Technique.

## 6. Drift, Overtaken and Saturated

You become what you do.

**Drift.** Keep a **Sin Tally**: one mark for each roll you tagged with a Sin, plus one for each scene you Rode a Sin. At the end of every **fourth session**, look at the tally. If your most-tagged Sin leads your second-most-tagged by **8 or more**, it gains +1 rating, to a maximum of 4. Your least-tagged Sin that you have at least 1 in (not the one that gained) loses 1. Ties: no drift for the gainer, you choose the loser. Then clear the tally. Your Alignment is whichever Sin is now highest. Total points stay constant; specializing is slow and always costs you somewhere else.

**Reversing drift.** A successful Reckoning (Part IV §7) that confronts the Sin you've been drifting toward lets you move one point back.

**Overtaken (rating 4).** Your Flashpoint Urge is always this Sin's Urge. Your Hail Mary results take this Sin's form. Your Signature Technique works twice per scene. Matchup swing is ±2 dice and Fit is +2 / −3. You are Under it whenever you are Unsteady or Empty: the less of yourself is left, the more the Sin drives.

**Saturated (rating 5).** Never reached by drift. At a Grade breakpoint (7, 5, 3, 1) or at the end of a Reckoning, you may choose to raise a rating-4 Sin to 5. It is a deliberate, risky choice. Matchup swing is ±3. You are **always Under** that Sin. You must tag that Sin at least once per scene or take +1 Stress at the end of it: you are starving. Everyone who knows you knows what beats you.

## 7. The Seven Sins in Detail

Each entry gives the wheel, the Fit, and everything the Sin adds to the Self. A *tag* is a roll tagged with the Sin; the Signature Technique needs rating 2 or more.

### Wrath

*Rage at what happened, or at whoever let it happen: a fire that burns indiscriminately.*

- **Wheel:** strong against Pride, Envy; weak against Sloth, Gloom.
- **Fit (when Under Wrath):** Favored Combat, Athletics, Persuasion; Hindered Stealth, Investigation, Medicine.
- **Signature condition: Burn.** Ongoing Hurt that persists a few rounds (at the end of each Exchange, a Burning target takes Hurt until it's put out).
- **Urge** (a Flashpoint): Lashes out first, hardest, at the nearest target - sometimes an ally.
- **Complication** (an E.G.O. die showing 1 or 2 on a Wrath-tagged roll): Collateral: something breaks, someone who wasn't the target is hurt, or a line is crossed in public.
- **Signature Technique, Rampage** (rating 2+, once per scene; rating 4+, twice): After a Wrath-tagged Success in a fight, make a second Wrath-tagged attack on a different target in the same Exchange at -1 die. If either roll shows a 1, an unintended person takes Hurt.
- **Manifestation cues:** Ember-lines along scars, heat haze, the smell of ozone, a carrying voice.
- **Distortion form:** Body becomes the weapon: heat, horns, armor of scar tissue, a voice that shakes walls.
- **The Return:** Someone stays, unflinching, and names what the rage cost them - being witnessed in the pain beneath it.

### Lust

*Longing and giving: the urge to spread a piece of yourself (a desire, a belief, what you know) and to hold on to whoever takes it.*

- **Wheel:** strong against Gloom, Sloth; weak against Gluttony, Envy.
- **Fit (when Under Lust):** Favored Persuasion, Empathy, Deception; Hindered Investigation, Technology, Corporate Knowledge.
- **Signature condition: Bleed.** A wound that reopens (each time a Bleeding target spends E.G.O. or acts under strain, it takes Hurt).
- **Urge** (a Flashpoint): Reaches for someone or something to cling to; overpromises; can't leave.
- **Complication** (an E.G.O. die showing 1 or 2 on a Lust-tagged roll): Attachment: a promise made in the moment, a new person pulled into the problem, or a Bond strained by what you offered.
- **Signature Technique, Hook** (rating 2+, once per scene; rating 4+, twice): On a Success on a Lust-tagged social roll, the target holds a 1-point Bond (Affection or Obligation) toward you until the scene ends, which you may spend once like a Bond of your own. The GM may veto if it crosses your Boundary.
- **Manifestation cues:** Warm light, perfume, mirrored faces, hands that linger.
- **Distortion form:** Binding and mimicry: entwined limbs, the faces of people loved or lost, a warmth that doesn't let go.
- **The Return:** Someone chooses them with nothing asked in return, or tells them they're allowed to let go.

### Sloth

*Numbness, hesitation, the freeze, going along with what others want.*

- **Wheel:** strong against Wrath, Pride; weak against Gloom, Lust.
- **Fit (when Under Sloth):** Favored Stealth, Medicine, Investigation; Hindered Athletics, Combat, Streetwise.
- **Signature condition: Tremor.** A delayed hit (a mark placed now bursts next Exchange for Harm, or earlier on a trigger).
- **Urge** (a Flashpoint): Stops. Waits. Defers the choice and lets someone else make it.
- **Complication** (an E.G.O. die showing 1 or 2 on a Sloth-tagged roll): Delay: the window closes, time is lost, an opportunity quietly passes.
- **Signature Technique, Unmoved** (rating 2+, once per scene; rating 4+, twice): Once per scene, as your Exchange action, do nothing: until your next action, any attack or pressure against you is rolled at -2 dice and you can't be Helped.
- **Manifestation cues:** Stillness, dust, a hush that spreads.
- **Distortion form:** Heavy and rooted: inertia fields, sleep, a body that won't move and won't let others move.
- **The Return:** A need that can't be deferred, voiced as a request rather than an order - something only they can answer, right now.

### Gluttony

*Hunger. Never enough.*

- **Wheel:** strong against Lust, Gloom; weak against Envy, Pride.
- **Fit (when Under Gluttony):** Favored Investigation, Streetwise, Corporate Knowledge; Hindered Stealth, Empathy, Medicine.
- **Signature condition: Rupture.** A Harm bonus on a marked target (each Rupture adds +1 Harm to the next hit, then fades).
- **Urge** (a Flashpoint): Takes more than needed - food, data, spoils, attention - and consumes it.
- **Complication** (an E.G.O. die showing 1 or 2 on a Gluttony-tagged roll): Excess: you took too much; it leaves a trace, a debt, a mess, or a rival who noticed.
- **Signature Technique, Devour** (rating 2+, once per scene; rating 4+, twice): Once per scene, when you inflict Harm or win a Social Conflict, regain 1 E.G.O. or take something (an object, a secret). You then owe the scene a cost: the GM names one.
- **Manifestation cues:** Wet sounds, swelling, the smell of something sweet going off.
- **Distortion form:** Maws and many mouths: swallowing, bloating, a body that makes room for whatever it takes.
- **The Return:** Being given something freely with nothing asked - the first time enough is enough.

### Gloom

*Grief, guilt, sorrow carried: sinking, and the deeper you go the harder it is to climb out. The one Sin with no deadly-sin twin.*

- **Wheel:** strong against Sloth, Wrath; weak against Lust, Gluttony.
- **Fit (when Under Gloom):** Favored Empathy, Medicine, Investigation; Hindered Persuasion, Deception, Athletics.
- **Signature condition: Sinking.** A growing penalty (each stack of Sinking removes a die from the target's next roll, to a maximum of -3).
- **Urge** (a Flashpoint): Collapses inward: takes the blame, apologizes, mourns aloud, drags others down.
- **Complication** (an E.G.O. die showing 1 or 2 on a Gloom-tagged roll): Weight: your sorrow is contagious - an ally nearby takes -1 die on their next roll.
- **Signature Technique, Sorrow's Weight** (rating 2+, once per scene; rating 4+, twice): On a Success on a Gloom-tagged roll, the target takes -2 dice on their next roll this scene (they feel it).
- **Manifestation cues:** Cold light, damp air, silence that turns to sobbing.
- **Distortion form:** Rain and water: drowned shapes, weeping figures, a body dissolving into what it grieves.
- **The Return:** Someone shares the weight and says the lost one's name with them.

### Pride

*Dignity and perfection - the need to be right, to be the machine that doesn't fail.*

- **Wheel:** strong against Envy, Gluttony; weak against Wrath, Sloth.
- **Fit (when Under Pride):** Favored Persuasion, Corporate Knowledge, Fixer Knowledge; Hindered Empathy, Stealth, Deception.
- **Signature condition: Poise.** Stacking readiness (each Poise adds +1 die to your next Pride roll; a Failure spends it all).
- **Urge** (a Flashpoint): Refuses help, doubles down, performs for the room.
- **Complication** (an E.G.O. die showing 1 or 2 on a Pride-tagged roll): Face: standing is lost, a witness saw you stumble, or someone now has something over your reputation.
- **Signature Technique, Unbowed** (rating 2+, once per scene; rating 4+, twice): Once per scene, after rolling, reroll up to three dice that didn't succeed. If the reroll still fails, take a Complication.
- **Manifestation cues:** Gold edges, a lifted chin, polished surfaces that reflect too much.
- **Distortion form:** A crown, a monument, armor of certainty: a thing that cannot bend and demands to be looked at.
- **The Return:** Being seen failing and still being valued - someone sees the flaw and stays anyway.

### Envy

*Comparison - inadequacy, resentment of someone else's success, a static shock of wanting what they have.*

- **Wheel:** strong against Gluttony, Lust; weak against Pride, Wrath.
- **Fit (when Under Envy):** Favored Deception, Stealth, Technology; Hindered Persuasion, Medicine, Fixer Knowledge.
- **Signature condition: Charge.** Stored force (spend Charge to add dice or Harm; unspent Charge discharges as a Complication at the end of the scene).
- **Urge** (a Flashpoint): Copies, undercuts, sabotages whoever is better.
- **Complication** (an E.G.O. die showing 1 or 2 on a Envy-tagged roll): Rivalry: someone becomes your rival, or covets what you have.
- **Signature Technique, Borrowed Face** (rating 2+, once per scene; rating 4+, twice): Once per scene, after your opponent tags a Sin, tag the same one yourself: the matchup is neutral and you add +1 die (you know exactly what they are).
- **Manifestation cues:** Pale green flicker, a face that isn't quite theirs, mimicked gestures.
- **Distortion form:** Stolen faces and borrowed shapes: a body that copies those around it, wearing everyone you've envied.
- **The Return:** Being recognized as themselves - named by someone who wants nothing from them.

## 8. Sins at the Table

**Alignment on everything.** Give each important NPC and each Abnormality an **Alignment** (a Sin). When the party meets one, the question becomes who counters it, who is weak to it, and how to talk to it. A *Sin:* line belongs in the NPC and Abnormality templates (Part VII, Part VIII).

> **EXAMPLE**
> *The Second Knock* (Part VIII §7) could be Sloth: it only acts when unobserved, and it stays put. Lust and Gloom are strong against it; Wrath and Pride do poorly.

**What to watch in play, and what to turn.** The Sin Layer is the least tested part of the book. If matchups are too decisive, drop the swing to ±1 for everyone. If Fit never bites, make the penalty land more often or require a cost to ride. If Drift feels like bookkeeping, lengthen it to every sixth session or drop it. If players tag for the bonus and not the fiction, make the GM veto more freely. The numbers behind the current values (and the full list of dials) are in the vault's Sin Layer playtest notes.
